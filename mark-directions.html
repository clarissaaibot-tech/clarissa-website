B · Root Tree

Organic crown with visible branches and a taproot. The canopy is one continuous scalloped curve — no triangles, no spikes — with branch lines showing through it. Below ground a single taproot splits into fine roots. Ember inside the crown. Reads as: rooted, with hidden structure below.

E · The Axis — form and exact reflection

"Mirror Streams" drawn literally. A hairline surface runs down the centre; the left side is the real form, the right side is its exact mirror image. The ember sits on the surface itself — the point where the two meet. This is the brand name as geometry, and it is the hardest of all of these for anyone else to use, because the reflection is not a stylistic choice, it is the idea.

Trade-offs

Strongest at

  • A — technical and architecture contexts; diagrams, decks, developer audiences
  • B — organisational and nature contexts; print, large surfaces, physical objects
  • E — anything about the brand itself: identity, the name, the mirror concept, the IP

Watch out for

  • A — reads as a molecule or diagram first; the tree is a second read
  • B — the organic crown is the hardest to keep consistent; needs careful curve work
  • E — the reflection must be pixel-exact or the whole point collapses; also two forms at once is busier
