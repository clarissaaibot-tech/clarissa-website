07 · Rules

Do

  • Keep the spark ember — it is the "question" and the tree is the system
  • Keep the cones flat-shaded (one lighter left face) — that is what makes it read as 3D
  • Use the reflection only in the primary lockup, never in the icon
  • Use the forest cluster to signal "no hierarchy"

Don't

  • Don't add a 4th cone — three is the tested geometry from the experiment
  • Don't round the cones into a blob or add leaf detail — it kills the low-poly signature
  • Don't put the tree above the mirror line — the tree is the real, the reflection is the idea
  • Don't use a second chromatic colour in the mark
  • Don't let the tree become "generic eco/nature brand" — the geometry is what makes it yours
