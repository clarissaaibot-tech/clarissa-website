Sources and a caveat

Philip W. Anderson, "More Is Different," Science (1972). A physicist's argument that whole systems behave in ways you can't read off their parts.

Friedrich Hayek, "The Use of Knowledge in Society," American Economic Review (1945). Why the knowledge an economy runs on can't be gathered into one head.

Daniel Dennett, Consciousness Explained (1991). The case that the brain has no central place where experience comes together.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching, chapter 17. Translations vary; the line here is a common rendering.

About the forest. Scientists disagree on how much trees really share through fungal networks. A 2023 review by Justine Karst, Melanie Jones and Jason Hoeksema in Nature Ecology & Evolution found that many popular claims go beyond the evidence. Here the network is a picture of coordination, not a claim about biology.
