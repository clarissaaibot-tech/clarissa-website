The path

Not a list of places.
A path with a direction.

Every map is an argument about what matters. This one says: a root, a long stay, and a slow widening of the world — and that the point was never the distance, but what each place taught about staying.

Some places here are still missing their story. That is deliberate. A map that invents its own memories is just decoration.
